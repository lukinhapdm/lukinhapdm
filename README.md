# 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Computer Engineer** graduated from **UNIFEI (Itabira Campus)** and a Mechatronics Technician graduated from **ETEP**. I have a solid background in software development, interface design, and project management.

I currently work as a **Full Stack Web Developer** at **Gecko Marketing**, where I architect and maintain digital ecosystems: CMS (WordPress and Elementor), server infrastructure, advanced tracking with **Google Tag Manager**, and customer service chatbots. At the same time, I am building an application with **Go (Golang), PostgreSQL, and Docker** to deepen my back-end, containerization, and data modeling skills.

For my thesis, I built a **healthcare chatbot with AI (RAG + LLM)** that answers questions grounded in official sources, and I keep studying **RAG, LLM fine-tuning, and prompt engineering** on my own.

Throughout my career, I have also worked as a developer and project manager in the junior companies **UP** and **ATLAS**, going from requirements modeling to interface development and the delivery of functional software.
